Plan the day.
Keep it yours.

Daymark is a calm planner for Android. To-dos, calendar, notes and habits live in one quiet app, and everything stays on your phone.

Download for Android
Free · Android 12 or newer · No account

Everything for the day, nothing extra

Five tabs, one plus button, and a soft frosted-glass look.

Today

What's overdue, what's on today in time order, your habits, and what's coming tomorrow.

To-dos

Folders, priorities, voice capture, due dates and repeats from daily to yearly.

Calendar

A week strip and a day timeline. Swipe between days and import your phone's calendars.

Notes

Pages with headings, checklists, tables, images and drawings, plus a cover image.

Saved

Share links, text and images from any app. Links get a title and preview, and you can comment on them.

Private

Hold Notes to open a section locked by your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

Habits you can see

Every day you show up becomes a coloured square.

  • Task or event. Tick it off whenever, or give it a set time and duration on your calendar.
  • Eight colours. Each habit wears its own, from the check button to its streak.
  • Month and year views. A calendar of squares, or a whole year like a contribution graph.
  • Streaks. Your current run and your best, in the app and on your home screen.
  • Reminders. A notification or an alarm, only on days you haven't checked in.

On your home screen

Nine widgets: tasks, calendar month, events, a pinned note, and habit month and year with a one-tap check-in.

Reminders that actually arrive

Any task, event or habit can send a notification with Done and Snooze, or ring like an alarm clock on the lock screen. Reminders come back after a restart, and Daymark tells you when a phone setting would block them.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and nowhere else. Daymark goes online for one thing only: fetching the title and picture of a link you save. Back up to a single file whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone.

Install

  1. On your Android phone, open the latest release and download the .apk file.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  3. To update later, install the newer APK over the old one. Your data stays.
